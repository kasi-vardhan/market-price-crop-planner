# Andhra Pradesh Crop Planning Context

## Source

Indian agricultural research and extension information.

## Planning

Crop suitability in Andhra Pradesh should be evaluated using:

- soil characteristics
- irrigation availability
- seasonal rainfall
- temperature
- crop season
- crop variety
- local market prices

Different districts can have substantially different soil,
rainfall and cropping patterns.

Therefore, a crop recommendation should not be generated from
state-level information alone when more local information is available.

## Market

Market prices can vary between agricultural markets.

The Market Price and Crop Planning Helper therefore uses government
market-price data separately from agricultural knowledge.

## Uncertainty

Historical conditions do not guarantee future weather, yield or market
prices.

The application should communicate uncertainty whenever important data
is missing or stale.
