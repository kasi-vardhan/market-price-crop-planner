# Groundnut Agricultural Knowledge

## Source

Organization: Indian Council of Agricultural Research

Source:
ICAR crop information and ICAR agricultural advisories.

## Soil

Groundnut generally benefits from well-drained soils.

ICAR agricultural advisory material specifically recommends well-drained
sandy and sandy-loam soils for groundnut and notes that heavy clay soils
are unsuitable because they interfere with peg development and
harvesting.

## Season

Groundnut is cultivated in kharif and can also be grown in other
production windows depending on region, irrigation and variety.

ICAR information identifies groundnut varieties recommended for kharif
cultivation in Andhra Pradesh among other states.

## Water

Groundnut generally requires less irrigation than water-intensive
paddy systems in some farming situations, although actual water
requirements depend on soil, rainfall, variety and crop stage.

## Planning principle

Suitability should consider:

- soil drainage
- soil texture
- irrigation
- seasonal rainfall
- temperature
- crop variety
- market conditions

Yield and profitability should not be guaranteed.
