# Tomato Agricultural Knowledge

## Source

Organization: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)

Source:
ICAR – Indian Institute of Horticultural Research / ICAR crop information

## Crop suitability

Tomato performance depends strongly on temperature, soil condition,
water availability, season and local growing conditions.

An ICAR report on tomato cultivation in Bishnupur, Manipur describes
optimal performance around 20–25°C and well-drained fertile loamy soil
with pH around 6.0–6.8 for the conditions described in that location.

These values should not be treated as universal requirements for every
tomato variety or every region.

## Season

Tomato can be cultivated in different seasons depending on location,
variety and production system.

ICAR examples show that planting windows vary by region and that
protected cultivation can allow production during periods that are
otherwise difficult for open-field cultivation.

## Important considerations

Tomato is sensitive to environmental stress.

Heavy rainfall and unsuitable temperature conditions can reduce
production, particularly in open-field cultivation.

Local variety selection and planting time should therefore be
considered along with soil, water and weather conditions.

## Recommendation principle

Crop planning should combine:

- local climate
- soil
- irrigation availability
- season
- crop variety
- current market conditions

The information in this document is intended as agricultural
decision-support knowledge and not as a guarantee of yield or profit.
