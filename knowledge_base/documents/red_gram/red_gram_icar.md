# Red Gram (Pigeonpea / Tur) Agricultural Knowledge

## Source
Organization: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR-IIPR) & ANGRAU
Source: ICAR - Indian Institute of Pulses Research / ANGRAU Pulses Production Guide

## Crop Suitability
Red gram (Cajanus cajan) is a deep-rooted, drought-tolerant legume crop.
Optimal growth temperature is 20°C to 35°C.
Performs well in well-drained red loamy soils, black cotton soils, and alluvial soils. Because of its deep taproot system (up to 2 meters), it can extract moisture from deeper subsoil layers during dry spells.

## Season and Sowing
- Kharif season crop: Sown in June to July.
- Medium to long duration crop (140–180 days).

## Water and Irrigation
Low to medium water requirement (500–900 mm).
Predominantly rainfed in peninsular India. If irrigation is available, protective watering at flowering and pod filling stages substantially improves pod setting. Sensitive to waterlogging; ridge and furrow planting is advised.

## Production and Economic Guidance
- Expected yield: 600–750 kg/acre (6–7.5 quintals/acre).
- Cultivation cost: ₹18,000–₹22,000 per acre.
- Mandi prices for Tur/Arhar have remained robust across Indian mandis due to strong domestic demand.

## Recommendation Principle
Excellent choice for kharif season on red or black soils under rainfed or moderate irrigation conditions, providing high protein grain, soil nitrogen enrichment, and resilient market value.
