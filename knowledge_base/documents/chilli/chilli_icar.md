# Chilli Agricultural Knowledge

## Source

Organization: Indian Council of Agricultural Research

Source:
ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025

## Crop

Chilli is cultivated under both irrigated and rainfed conditions.

## Season

ICAR's 2025 Kharif advisory describes May–June as an ideal sowing
period for kharif chilli in the relevant advisory context.

For irrigated cultivation, other planting windows may be used depending
on region and production system.

## Irrigation

Chilli production can be carried out under irrigated and rainfed
conditions, but water management must be adapted to the local climate
and crop stage.

## Crop management

ICAR provides recommendations covering:

- seed rate
- seed treatment
- transplanting
- spacing
- fertilizer management
- crop protection

These recommendations are variety-, region- and production-system
dependent.

## Planning principle

The crop planner should consider:

- soil
- irrigation availability
- season
- local climate
- variety
- market conditions

Agricultural recommendations should not be interpreted as guaranteed
yield or profit.
