# Paddy Agricultural Knowledge

## Source

Organization:
Indian Council of Agricultural Research

## Crop

Rice/paddy is widely cultivated under irrigated and rainfed systems
across different regions of India.

## Water

Paddy generally has substantially higher water requirements than
many drought-tolerant crops.

Water availability is therefore an important factor when comparing
paddy with crops such as groundnut.

## Soil

Rice can be cultivated across different soil conditions, but local
soil characteristics, water availability, drainage and cultivation
system affect suitability.

## Season

Rice planting and harvesting windows vary significantly between
agro-climatic regions.

Regional crop calendars should therefore be preferred over one
universal planting date.

## Planning principle

The crop planner should evaluate:

- water availability
- soil type
- season
- seasonal rainfall
- local climate
- variety
- market conditions

The recommendation system must not guarantee yield or profit.
